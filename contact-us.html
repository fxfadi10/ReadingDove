Frequently Asked Questions

How do I suggest a book for review?

Use the contact form above and select "Book Suggestion" as the subject. Include the book title, author, and why you think it deserves a spot on ReadingDove.

Are your book reviews unbiased?

Absolutely. While we earn affiliate commissions, our reviews are 100% honest and independent. We only recommend books we genuinely believe in.

Can I advertise on ReadingDove?

Yes! We offer advertising opportunities including sponsored content and banner placements. Select "Advertising" in the contact form for details.

Do you accept guest posts?

We occasionally accept high-quality guest contributions from book experts and authors. Reach out via the form with your pitch and writing samples.

How do I update my newsletter preferences?

Every newsletter email includes an "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom. You can also email us at hello@readingdove.com to update your preferences.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet, but our website is fully responsive and works great on mobile devices. An app is on our roadmap for late 2026!
