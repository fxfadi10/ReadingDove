Our Story

ReadingDove was born from a simple frustration: too many books, too little time, and no reliable way to find the gems worth reading. In 2024, a small team of passionate readers set out to build the book recommendation site we wished existed.

What started as a humble blog reviewing business and self-help books quickly grew into a trusted destination for readers across every genre. Today, ReadingDove serves thousands of book lovers monthly, helping them cut through the noise and find stories that truly resonate.

We believe great books have the power to transform lives, spark new ideas, and connect people across cultures and generations. That's why we take every recommendation seriously — because your next favorite book could change everything.
